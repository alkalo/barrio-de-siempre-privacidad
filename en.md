# Privacy policy for "Hometown Forever"

Last updated: 25-11-2026 · [Versión en español](es.html)

"Hometown Forever" ("Barrio de Siempre" in Spanish) is a puzzle game for Android phones. This policy explains, in plain words, what data the game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

## Summary

- **We do not ask for or keep your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or exact location.**
- **No ads and no real-money purchases.** We do not use your phone's advertising ID.
- Your game progress is saved **only on your phone**.
- Only if you **agree** on the privacy screen, the game sends **anonymous gameplay data** (for example, "level 12 won in 40 moves") and **crash reports**, to improve the game. You can withdraw at any time in **Settings → Privacy**.

## Who is responsible

Alejandro Casanova López ("alkalo" on Google Play), the game's developer and data controller. Contact: **hometownforeverhelp@gmail.com**.

## What data is used

| Data | Where it is stored | Why | Needs your permission? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Game progress (levels, stars, coins, restored neighbourhood, settings) | Only on your phone | So you can continue where you left off | No: it never leaves the phone |
| Anonymous gameplay data: events such as starting or finishing a level, tutorial steps, use of helpers, coins earned or spent, restored areas, settings changes | Google Firebase Analytics | To learn which levels are too hard, where people get stuck and how long they play | **Yes**, and it is off by default |
| Technical data Firebase adds by itself: a random install ID, phone model, Android and game version, language, approximate country or region | Google Firebase | Same as above, and to count distinct players without knowing who they are | **Yes** (same anonymous data as above) |
| Crash reports: which part of the game failed, phone model and version | Google Firebase Crashlytics | To fix unexpected closes | **Yes** (same permission) |

The game **never** sends text you type, your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. Google receives your connection's IP address when data is sent, uses it to work out the approximate country, and does not store it with the data.

## Legal basis

Anonymous gameplay data and crash reports are sent only with your **consent** (Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation). If you say no, the game works exactly the same.

## Who it is shared with

Only **Google** (Firebase), which processes the data on our behalf as a data processor. Google may process it outside the European Union under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. More information: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We never sell data.

## How long it is kept

- Anonymous gameplay data: **2 months** in Firebase, then deleted automatically.
- Crash reports: **90 days**.
- Game progress on your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Your rights and how to delete your data

You can withdraw consent, access, delete or object to the use of your data at any time:

1. **In the game (immediate):** **Settings → Privacy → "Stop sharing and delete my data"**. The game stops sending data, deletes the local copy and asks Firebase to delete the data linked to your install.
2. **By email:** write to **hometownforeverhelp@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my data". Since we don't know who you are, include the **player code** shown in **Settings → Privacy**. We reply within **30 days**.
3. **Uninstalling the game** deletes the progress saved on your phone.

You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or the authority in your country.

## Children

The game is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, if the change affects your data, ask you again in the game.
